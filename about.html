A bit more about me…

I graduated from WashU in St. Louis May 2026 with my BA in Design: Communications concentration and a minor in computer science. Before transferring into the Sam Fox school of Spring 2024, I was pursuing a degree in Cognitive Neuroscience and took numerous psychology courses as well as religious studies and philosophy of religion courses. Throughout my four years at WashU, I took creative writing courses and was an active member of the Ghost Lights acapella group in order to cultivate my creative interests outside of design. While I ultimately took web and UX specific courses towards the end of my undergraduate career, I loved gaining experience in typography, typesetting, book cover design, book making and letterpress printing.

Outside of academics, I love singing, songwriting and modeling for stylists and editorial magazines. You can often find me with a coffee or matcha in hand, listening to music on Spotify (and if I am not actively working, scrolling through Pinterest for home decor inspiration.)

I plan on continuing my education at WashU, and will be attending the MDes for HCI and Emerging Technology program Fall 2027 - May 2029. During my gap year, I hope to gain more real-world design experience, grow a variety of my creative skills whether design related or not, and continue to explore the beautiful city of St. Louis.
